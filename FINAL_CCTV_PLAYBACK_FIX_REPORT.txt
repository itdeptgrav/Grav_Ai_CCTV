==========================================================================================
ISSUE 1 -- RECORDED AUDIO
==========================================================================================
1. Exact reason recorded audio did not play
   The audio path itself worked (NVR -> server -> WebSocket -> G.711 decoded in the
   browser -> Web Audio). Three things made it look broken:
   a) The screenshot was at 4x. In fast playback the NVRs send NO audio at all --
      measured at the RTSP level: 0 audio packets in 6 s at Scale 2.0 and 4.0 on both
      NVRs (while video key frames kept coming). The server dropped audio at 2x/4x and
      the page DISABLED the speaker, yet the tile still said "Audio: PCMU" -- misleading.
   b) At 1x the sound was off until a small speaker icon in the tile footer was
      clicked; there was no speaker/volume in the controls and no "click to listen"
      prompt (the volume only appeared after that click).
   c) The PCMU cameras (NVR1) record extremely quietly. Measured at 1x (28 Sep, office
      hours): CEO office typical -51..-56 dBFS, Reception -50, Meeting -47, corridor
      -58 dBFS (its loudest 40 ms only -48). The old player could add at most +6 dB
      (volume 100 % = 2x), so it played at about -50 dBFS -- practically inaudible.
      NVR2 (PCMA) cameras record at -19..-40 dBFS: audible.
   Fix: speaker (listen / mute / unmute) + volume + a status line in the controls,
   the speaker per camera kept, explicit states, audio kept selected through fast
   playback and resumed at 1x by itself, and an automatic level for quiet
   microphones (up to +30 dB towards -24 dBFS, limiter against loud events).
2. Does 1x real audio now work?            PASS (see 4 for the human-ear part)
   Browser test (click on the speaker): AudioContext running; packets at real time
   (PCMA 8/s x 1024 B, PCMU 25.7/s x 320 B); level measured at the speaker output
   (analyser after boost + volume): PM office -16..-33 dBFS, CEO office -23..-29 dBFS
   ("quiet microphone raised +30 dB"). Status e.g. "Playing · CEO office · -23 dB ·
   quiet microphone raised +30 dB". Also after a seek to another day, pause/resume,
   mute/unmute, and 4x -> 1x (sound back after 0.6 s) -- no page reload.
3. Audio codecs tested:                    PCMA 8 kHz (NVR2), PCMU 8 kHz (NVR1)
4. Actual sound heard:                     NOT TESTED by ear (I cannot hear). The
   signal at the browser's audio output was measured (above). 20 s WAV clips of the
   real recordings were sent: CEO_office_PCMU_as_recorded.wav (nearly silent -- the
   cause), CEO_office_PCMU_as_played_auto_level.wav (what the player now outputs),
   PM_office_PCMA_as_recorded.wav. Please confirm by clicking the speaker at 1x.
5. 2x audio behaviour:  no sound -- NOT POSSIBLE from these NVRs (0 audio packets at
   Scale 2). Shown explicitly: "Audio plays at 1× only (fast playback has no sound)";
   tile "Audio at 1× only (PCMU)"; the choice is kept; sound returns at 1x by itself.
   Accelerated audio is not practical: the only source of 2x/4x footage is the NVR's

11. Range-splitting implementation
   * playback_time.plan_ranges(a, b, hours): consecutive NVR-safe windows (local time,
     across midnight fine). New limit: 31 days (a month covers what the NVRs keep;
     justified by the 30-day search test) -- CCTV_PLAYBACK_MAX_RANGE_H.
     UPDATE (follow-up below): the limit now follows each NVR's OLDEST recording; the
     31 days only apply while that is not known yet.
   * Search: split into requests of <= 7 days (CCTV_PLAYBACK_SEARCH_CHUNK_H; one 7-day
     request is 4-5x faster than seven 1-day ones), pages of 100 files, results
     deduplicated and merged into ONE timeline (gaps kept). Metadata only; one query
     at a time per NVR (never parallel queries on an NVR), the cameras of the visible
     page first. The reply comes within 3 s; slower results reach the page over its
     WebSocket and cameras start playing as their result arrives.
   * Playback: NVR sessions of <= 24 h (CCTV_PLAYBACK_SESSION_SPAN_H; the NVRs cover
     ~58 h). At a session's end playback continues in the next session by itself (no
     Search click); a seek outside the current session opens the right one (~3 s).
   * Page: one timeline over all days -- dated ticks ("26 Sep 10:00:00"), midnight
     lines, dated seek bubble, "searching…" rows, Shift+arrow = 10 min.

==========================================================================================
FOLLOW-UP (28 Sep, 17:10-18:10) -- THE LIMIT FOLLOWS EACH NVR'S OLDEST RECORDING
==========================================================================================
Before: a fixed 31 days (CCTV_PLAYBACK_MAX_RANGE_H) -- our own number, not an NVR limit.
Now a search reaches back exactly as far as the selected cameras' NVRs still have footage
(their disks are full and overwrite the oldest footage all the time).
  * The server reads each NVR's OLDEST recording at start and every 30 min
    (CCTV_PLAYBACK_RETENTION_REFRESH_S). Office LAN: the web API's file list -- one
    metadata request per camera, 30-day windows (the query size measured safe), stepping
    further back only if an NVR ever keeps more. Outside the office: RTSP DESCRIBE checks
    (bisection, then the exact second; ~80-100 short requests per NVR, one at a time),
    taken 10 s early so it is never later than any camera's oldest footage. A failed
    check keeps the last good answer and is retried after 2 min.
  * Search: From earlier than the oldest recording -> the search starts there, with a
    note ("Nothing is recorded before 01 Sep 2026 18:00:00, so the search starts there.
    NVR1 keeps 27 days of footage (from 01 Sep 2026 18:00)."); a range entirely before it
    -> "No recordings that old: ...". Cameras on both NVRs: the older NVR bounds it. The
    fixed 31 days apply ONLY while an NVR's oldest recording is not known yet (the first
    12-42 s after a server start, or an NVR that never answered) -- the message says so.
  * Page: "footage kept: NVR1 27 days (from 1 Sep 18:00), NVR2 22.5 days (from 6 Sep
    05:00)" next to the time zone; the date pickers start at the oldest date of the
    selected cameras' NVRs; the same checks before sending; the note above the timeline;
    refreshed every 10 min and with every search. Settings > Recorded playback shows
    "footage kept: 27 days (oldest recording 2026-09-01 18:00)" per NVR.
